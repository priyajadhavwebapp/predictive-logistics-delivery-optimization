# Phase 11 – Expected Outcomes and Business Impact

## Expected Outcomes
The project is expected to:
- Measure delivery performance through KPIs.
- Identify patterns associated with delivery delays.
- Compare logistics performance across regions and transportation modes.
- Provide a foundation for delivery-time prediction.
- Identify operational shipment segments.
- Provide a structured basis for transportation/resource optimization.

## Potential Business Impact
The analysis can support:
- Better shipment planning
- Improved resource allocation
- Delivery-performance monitoring
- Identification of delay-prone operations
- Evidence-based transportation decisions
- Future predictive logistics systems

## Limitations
- Public datasets may not contain real-time GPS or traffic information.
- Weather data may be unavailable.
- Historical data may not represent future conditions.
- Results depend on data quality and available features.
