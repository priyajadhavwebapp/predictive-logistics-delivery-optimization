# Phase 1 – Project Definition

## Project Title
**Predictive Logistics and Delivery Performance Optimization Using Python**

## Logistics Scenario
LogiTrack Logistics Pvt. Ltd. is a logistics and supply chain company responsible for transporting and delivering products across different cities in India. The company manages shipments involving different destinations, quantities, transportation modes, distances, and delivery schedules.

As shipment volume increases, the company faces delivery delays, variations in delivery time, inefficient resource allocation, and differences in logistics performance across regions.

## Problem Statement
The company wants to use historical logistics data and data science techniques to identify factors affecting delivery performance, predict delivery time, and support better transportation resource allocation.

## Research Question
How can data science techniques be used to analyze logistics data, predict delivery performance, identify potential delays, and support efficient transportation resource allocation?

## Scope
- Shipment and delivery performance analysis
- Delivery-delay analysis
- KPI measurement
- Exploratory data analysis
- Delivery-time prediction
- Shipment clustering
- Optimization strategy planning
