# Phase 2 – Project Objectives and KPI Identification

## Objectives
1. Analyze historical shipment and delivery data using Python.
2. Identify factors influencing delivery time and delays.
3. Measure logistics performance using KPIs.
4. Compare delivery performance across transportation modes and regions.
5. Use EDA and visualization to identify logistics patterns.
6. Propose regression for delivery-time prediction.
7. Propose clustering for shipment segmentation.
8. Develop optimization strategies for resource allocation.
9. Provide insights for logistics decision-making.

## KPIs

| KPI | Formula / Definition | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| On-Time Delivery Rate | On-time deliveries / Total deliveries × 100 | Delivery reliability |
| Average Delivery Time | Total delivery time / Number of deliveries | Delivery speed |
| Average Delay | Total delay / Delayed shipments | Delay severity |
| Order Fulfillment Rate | Delivered orders / Total orders × 100 | Fulfillment performance |
| Cancellation Rate | Cancelled orders / Total orders × 100 | Order reliability |

## Proposed Planning Targets
- On-Time Delivery Rate: ≥ 90%
- Average Delivery Time: Reduce by 10%
- Average Delay: Reduce by 15%
- Fulfillment Rate: ≥ 95%
- Cancellation Rate: < 5%

These are proposed project targets, not real-company measurements.

## Business Questions
1. What percentage of shipments are delivered on time?
2. What is the average delivery time?
3. Which transportation modes have higher delivery times?
4. Which regions experience more delays?
5. What shipment characteristics are associated with longer delivery times?
6. Can delivery time be predicted?
7. Can shipments be meaningfully segmented?
8. How can logistics resources be allocated more efficiently?
